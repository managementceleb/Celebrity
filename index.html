The House

We are not a fan club. We are a house of representation — and every card we issue carries the weight of a signature.

LUXE exists for the people who follow closely and follow well. Each card is a formal instrument: registered on-chain, numbered to its holder, and honoured for life. It is not sold twice, not transferred to strangers, and not reissued when it can be avoided. When the house opens a new name to the roster, we open it slowly.
